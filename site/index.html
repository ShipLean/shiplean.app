Why ShipLean

Planning should take minutes, not meetings.

Most planning tools grew into everything for everyone. ShipLean does backlogs, boards and sprints well, keeps every screen quiet and dense, and leaves the rest of the day for building.

One ranked backlog

Every sprint is the same backlog order filtered by sprint, so rank means the same thing everywhere. Drag a story into a sprint to plan it; drag it out to unplan it.

A forecast you can trust

Velocity comes from what the team actually finished. ShipLean fills each future sprint's free room first, then shows where the rest of the backlog lands.

Work is pulled, not pushed

Capacity is planned for the team as a whole, never person by person. Pick up a task and it's yours; move it to In Progress and it says who's on it.

Tour

Six screens, one backlog.

Planning, the sprint, the taskboard and capacity are all views over the same work items, so nothing is ever copied between them.

Plan the next few sprints in one list

The current sprint, the future sprints and the backlog, stacked in one ranked list.

  • Drag between sprints, or Alt+↑/↓ from the keyboard
  • Committed against team capacity on every sprint
  • A forecast line for each sprint the backlog reaches

Everything about this sprint on one screen

The goal, the burndown and the stories with their tasks, plus what's ready for someone to pick up.

  • Burndown against an ideal line
  • Change state in place from the list
  • Ready to pick up and In progress now, side by side

Run the sprint on a live taskboard

One row per story or bug, one column per task state, updated as the team works.

  • Group by stories or by people
  • Pick up a task in one click
  • Finished stories fold down to a single line

Capacity for the team, not a timesheet

Each person enters their own points. Only the team total is compared with the sprint.

  • A note for the low weeks, like Off Thu–Fri
  • A sense check against velocity
  • Copy last sprint's numbers in one click

Velocity from what really happened

Every sprint with its goal, capacity, committed and completed points, newest first.

  • Committed vs completed for the last five sprints
  • A new sprint with sensible defaults
  • Overlapping dates caught before they're saved

Close the sprint without the shuffle

When a sprint ends with work unfinished, ShipLean asks once where each item should go.

  • Next sprint, top of the backlog, or leave it
  • A warning if the next sprint goes over
  • History and velocity stay exactly as they were
Forecast

Know what fits before you promise it.

ShipLean fills the room left in each planned sprint, then plans the rest of the backlog at your real velocity. A line under the last story that fits shows where each sprint ends, and it moves the moment someone re-ranks.

Taskboard

Make the right cards stand out.

Choose which fields every card shows, colour tags, and add style rules like tagged Blocked or waiting on my review. The first matching rule wins, and every open board picks up the change straight away.

Roll over in one decision.

Unfinished stories don't quietly stay behind or pile into the next sprint. The first person to open the board after the end date chooses where each one goes, sees what that does to the next sprint, and applies it for everyone.
